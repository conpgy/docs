KeyKeep is an offline password manager that stores data locally on your device. If you experience a problem while using the app, please review the frequently asked questions below or contact us by email.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I need to create an account?

No. KeyKeep does not require account registration and does not depend on a cloud account.

2. Where are my passwords stored?

Passwords, accounts, cards and other private information you save are stored in an encrypted database on your current device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

3. Does KeyKeep support cloud sync?

The current version does not provide cloud synchronisation or online backup. Data stored on different devices remains separate.

4. What happens if I forget my unlock password?

KeyKeep does not store your unlock password in plain text, and the developer cannot access, recover or reset it. If you forget your unlock password, you may no longer be able to access the contents of your existing vault.

5. Why is biometric unlock unavailable?

Make sure your Mac supports the relevant biometric feature and that it has been configured in System Settings. If biometric unlock is unavailable or disabled, you can still access your vault using your unlock password.

6. How do I report a problem?

When contacting us, please include the following information where possible:

  • Your KeyKeep version;
  • Your macOS version;
  • The steps that led to the problem;
  • Any error message or privacy-redacted screenshot.
Do not send real passwords, database files, your unlock password, database keys or other sensitive information by email.
