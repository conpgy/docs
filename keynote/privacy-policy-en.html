Welcome to 小钥记 (the “App”). The App is developed and operated by 彭根勇 (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

We value your privacy and the security of your personal information. The App is an offline password manager. It does not provide account registration, cloud synchronization, advertising, user behavior analytics, or remote data analytics. Content you save in the App is stored and processed only on your device. We do not upload this content to our servers and cannot view, recover, or restore it.

Please read this Policy carefully before using the App. If you have any questions, you may contact us using the details in Section 9.

1. How We Process Your Information

1.1 Vault Content You Choose to Save

To provide password-management features, the App processes information that you enter or save locally on your device, including:

  • Vault names;
  • Entry titles, usernames, passwords, website addresses, and notes; and
  • Favorite status, usage count, and creation and modification times.

This content may include personal information or sensitive personal information. It is processed solely to provide on-device features for creating, saving, searching, editing, organizing, and deleting password entries. The content is stored in the App’s local encrypted database and is not uploaded to our servers.

Do not store another person’s personal information in the App unless you are authorized or otherwise have a lawful basis to process it.

1.2 Unlock and Security Information

To protect your local vault, the App processes the following information locally on your device:

  • The App unlock password you set;
  • Cryptographic material derived from the unlock password, as well as a randomly generated database key, salt, nonce, and authentication information;
  • Whether biometric unlock is enabled, the number of failed attempts, and the authentication result returned by the operating system; and
  • Security settings such as the automatic-lock interval.

The App does not store your unlock password in plaintext. The database key is cryptographically protected and stored in secure storage provided by the operating system. We cannot obtain or recover your unlock password. If you forget it, you may be unable to access your existing vault content.

1.3 Biometric Information

If you choose to enable fingerprint, Face ID, Touch ID, or another system biometric feature, the App invokes the authentication capability provided by your operating system. The App receives only results such as “success,” “failure,” or “canceled,” along with the types of biometric authentication supported by the device. The App does not read, store, or upload your fingerprint, facial features, or other biometric templates. Those templates are managed by your device and operating system.

You may disable biometric unlock at any time in the App’s settings or manage the relevant permissions or credentials in your device settings. After disabling it, you can continue to access the App with your unlock password.

1.4 Preferences

To preserve your choices, the App stores preferences such as language, automatic-lock interval, favorites filter status, biometric setting, and failed-attempt count locally on your device. These settings are not uploaded to our servers.

1.5 Local Operational Logs

To help diagnose operational failures, the App records limited events and error information locally on your device, such as database open or close status, successful or failed unlock attempts, biometric invocation status, and exception information. The logs do not intentionally record your saved passwords, usernames, entry content, unlock password, or database key. However, exception information generated by the system or a dependency may contain technical details such as local file paths or database-operation context.

Logs remain only on your device and are generally retained for seven days, after which the App automatically removes them. We do not receive these logs automatically. We receive only content that you choose to send us, such as logs or screenshots submitted by email. Before sending anything, please review and redact information you do not wish to disclose.

1.6 Basic App and Device Information

The App reads its version number locally to display the current version on the settings screen. We do not upload device identifiers, advertising identifiers, telephone numbers, contacts, precise location, or similar information to our servers.

2. Network Access and Disclosure

The App’s core features do not require network access. We do not operate servers that receive your vault content, and we do not sell, rent, exchange, share, or transfer outside your country or region any content you save in the App.

The App may invoke another application on your device in the following situations initiated by you:

  • When you tap a website address in a password entry, the App passes the address to your system’s default browser. The browser and destination website may process the web address, IP address, device information, and other data under their respective privacy policies.
  • When you tap “Feedback,” the App opens your system email client and pre-fills the recipient address and subject. The email service provider and we receive the message body, sender address, and any attachments or other content you choose to include only after you confirm and send the email.

These third-party applications and services are operated independently by their respective providers, and their data practices are not controlled by this Policy. Please review their privacy policies before use.

5. Storage Period, Location, and Security

  1. Storage location: Content you save in the App, cryptographic key material, preferences, and operational logs are stored locally on the device on which you use the App. We do not upload them to our servers or actively transfer them outside your country or region.
  2. Storage period: Vault content and preferences remain until you delete them in the App, clear the App’s data, or otherwise remove them. Operational logs are generally retained for seven days and are automatically removed after that period.
  3. Security measures: The App uses a local encrypted database, operating-system secure storage, key derivation, and authenticated encryption to protect data. It also supports automatic locking and optional biometric unlock.
  4. Risk notice: No security measure can guarantee absolute security. A compromised, jailbroken, or rooted device, malware, failure of operating-system security, or disclosure of your unlock password may increase the risk of data exposure. Use a strong and unique unlock password and protect your device.
  5. Backup notice: The App itself does not provide cloud backup or cross-device recovery. We take reasonable steps to prevent system backups of App data on supported platforms, but backups created by the device operating system, migration tools, or users may depend on the relevant platform settings. Secure your devices and backup media.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

The App’s data is stored only on your device. We cannot remotely access, retrieve, correct, copy, or delete it. You can manage it yourself in the following ways:

  • View, modify, or delete an individual password entry in the App;
  • Delete a vault you created and its contents;
  • Disable biometric unlock or change the automatic-lock setting in Settings; and
  • Use the operating system’s “Clear App Data” feature or uninstall the App to remove data from the App sandbox.

Please note that on some Apple devices, Keychain data may be retained by the operating system after the App is uninstalled. If you want to remove the relevant data completely, disable biometric unlock before uninstalling and use the options provided by the operating system to clear App or Keychain data. Deletion may be irreversible, so please confirm before proceeding.

If you have voluntarily sent us feedback by email, you may use the contact details in Section 9 to request access, correction, or deletion. After verifying your identity, we will process or respond to your request within 15 business days, unless applicable law provides otherwise.

7. Protection of Minors

The App is not primarily directed to children under 14. Because the App does not require registration and processes data only on the device, we generally cannot determine a user’s age. Minors should use the App under the guidance of a parent or other guardian. A child under 14 should obtain consent from a parent or other guardian before using the App. Guardians should help minors avoid storing unnecessary sensitive information about themselves or others.
